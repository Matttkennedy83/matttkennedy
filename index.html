About Matthew

Experience earned by building, not observing.

I have spent more than two decades working in and around trade-based businesses, navigating the same challenges that confront most business owners: winning work, building capable teams, improving systems, managing cash flow and maintaining profitability through growth.

That journey included helping grow a startup trade business into an operation producing more than $30 million in annual turnover. Along the way, I developed a practical understanding of what enables a business to scale—and what can quietly hold it back.

Today, alongside my ongoing business and investment interests, I am the founder of Growth Reset, a business mentoring and advisory practice helping owners of established trade and service businesses navigate growth, leadership and operational complexity.

“The best business advice is useful on Monday morning—not just impressive in a boardroom.”

Business Philosophy

Five principles I keep returning to.

01

Think long term

Good decisions compound. Build for enduring value rather than a short-term result.

02

Build great people

Businesses improve when their leaders create clarity, trust and accountability.

03

Create systems

Reliable systems reduce dependence on individuals and make growth repeatable.

04

Protect cash

Revenue creates excitement, profit creates confidence and cash creates resilience.

05

Keep improving

Small, consistent improvements are often more valuable than occasional dramatic change.
